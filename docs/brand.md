# ForGrow brand basics

## Identity anchors (must appear in every creative — this is what makes it recognisable)
- The founder's ORIGINAL logo (speech bubble with three bars + "ForGrow"): brand/logo.png (white, transparent),
  brand/logo_icon.png (bubble only). Never redraw it or replace it with typed text.
  On light or busy backgrounds place the logo on a navy (#101B4F) rounded tile so it stays as supplied.
- Signature mint accent #14D8A5 used at least once (highlight word, CTA button or key mark).
- Heebo typeface for Hebrew.
- Supporting colours from the logo: navy #101B4F / #14246A / #0A1238, periwinkle #8799DB, lavender #C5CBEE, white.

## What may vary between creatives (founder: style need not copy the logo, identity must be clear)
- Background (navy, light, photo-like illustration), composition, illustration type, motion style.
- Never two creatives in a row with the same treatment; changing only colour or headline is not a new creative.

## Message
- The business value of a relevant, organic audience: people discover the business, follow, keep seeing its
  content, understand the offer, and may later reach out or buy. Every ad = a clear business problem, the value,
  and ONE action.
- Never: promised followers or sales, fixed follower-to-customer ratios, "no risk / no blocks", Meta approval.
- Describe ForGrow only as approved: "ForGrow נבנה כדי לעזור לעסקים להגיע באינסטגרם לקהל רלוונטי — ולמדוד מה
  קורה אחר כך", "בפיילוט", "בלי קניית עוקבים". Anything more needs founder approval.
- Illustrative people/businesses are fictional and labelled "המחשה". No fake chats, reviews, results or dashboards.

## Music
- Default: original music generated by pipeline/music.py (no third-party material → no licence to clear).
- Library music only from a source whose licence explicitly covers the use (organic AND paid ads), recorded in the spec.
- Instagram's in-app music is not assumed to be licensed for ads.
